4The GROW 2026 application

Eighty-one people at thirty-two companies, written between 4 and 14 August. The application needs three seats filled by 1 September: a Dutch company to hold it, a Dutch knowledge institution, and an industrial partner whose operation it is built around.

Deltares. They apply for GROW subsidies often and have run many joint industry projects that way. On this one they said an application needs careful preparation and a co-investment, and they do not start new applications a few weeks before a deadline. They asked to learn more anyway. A meeting was held with Remon Pot and two colleagues. No concrete way forward yet, and an NDA is going to be signed.

RWE. An Stroobandt checked internally with their innovation department and the project team: they will not take part in the GROW project, because they have other projects running in the same area and already use a range of forecasting and decision-support tools. She said RWE is interested in the subject itself. A reply went back on 14 August pointing at RWE's own OranjeWind technology report, where strong sea currents at the site are named as a project challenge, and it went to three further RWE people. No answer yet.

Seaway7 (four people), IQIP (two) and Huisman (two) were all written on 5 and 6 August and none has answered. A second follow-up was prepared for them and has not gone out. Whether they are due one is an open question.
